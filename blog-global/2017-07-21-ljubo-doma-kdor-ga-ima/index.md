When our aeroplane landed in France, we felt at home, even though we were only in France. Thank goodness for the European Union. Most of us agreed that we had had enough of America for a while. There must be something in the saying that you have to go abroad to appreciate what you have at home.
<!-- truncate -->

_The team that represented Slovenia at the FIRST Global Challenge 2017 consisted of Vegova Ljubljana students Luka Maček, Mark Breznik and David Kraševec, with their teacher Aleš Volčini as mentor. Two further Vegova students, Jernej Možina Macarol and Gašper Hribar, also helped to build the robot. The students were selected, and prepared for interviews and presentations in English, by their English teacher, Mrs Vida Vidmar. The initiative to take part in the competition came from Rok Capuder of Zavod 404, which provided the equipment and workspace. Advice, technical assistance and help with the machinery were provided by Matevž Zorec and the other staff of Zavod 404._
