‘Thank you!’ is the word of the day. The fourth challenge, called ‘Volunteer Shoutout’, is all about us students showing our appreciation to our mentors and to the volunteers of FIRST for all their hard work, patience, support and warm thoughts. <!-- truncate --> Hidden within the video there is also a little Easter egg – did you manage to find it? Well, then do have another look 😀 Our mascot Pikachu is grateful too, albeit in a rather quieter way, and thanks everyone for all the hard work!

“To all of you, who have worked Hard, so that young engineers Across the world can experience
something New. this message goes out to all the volunteers and mentors, for all their Kindness,
warm thought, patience and the support of the Youth, that will hopefully carry on the
tradition of STEM and FIRST. Of course a big thank you and nice job goes out to all of Us
young engineers, together we will make it and thank you once more.”
