The Lost Robot

July 14, 2017 · 6 min read
Aleš Volčini

The FIRST Global Challenge 2017 would probably have passed unnoticed by all eyes and ears, had the American administration not blocked visas for the team from Afghanistan, made up entirely of girls, and for the team from the Gambia. The news was carried by every major news outlet in the world and so drew attention to the fact that such a competition existed at all. The visas were granted only after the intervention of the American President, Donald Trump, who reportedly took up the matter personally. Slovenia also took part in the competition, represented by a team consisting of David Kraševec, Luka Maček and Mark Breznik, students of Vegova Ljubljana, and one of their mentors, Aleš Volčini, who teaches technical subjects there and who wrote this short diary.
