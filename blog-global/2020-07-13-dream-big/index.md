This year’s first challenge, from a somewhat less technological field, is called ‘Dream Big’. In it we showed our highest highs and lowest lows, from which we learnt a great deal.
<!-- truncate --> Our submission is a video montage of our team members’ projects, highlighting their greatest successes and failures, together with our speech about dreams, set to a rendition of Welcome to the Machine by Pink Floyd performed by one of our mentors, who has asked us to point out that he has been playing the guitar for only a few months and is still learning.
