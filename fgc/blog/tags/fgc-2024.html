A Snapping Mechanism, Exploding Kittens and Clattering Soldiers’ Shoes

September 28, 2024 · 9 min read
David Zindović

Saturday had arrived, our penultimate day in the Greek capital. The morning ritual was followed by a generous helping of work, as the robot had to be put in order before the first match of the day, which was not very long after the hour of our arrival. Unfortunately the work with the screwdrivers dragged on to such an extent that we played one match without a robot, with only our human player representing the team. So it was that we ended match number 123, in the company of Argentina and Cape Verde, against the line-up of Saint Vincent and the Grenadines, the Maldives and Sudan, with a defeat (16–58).

First Points in Greece

September 27, 2024 · 5 min read
David Zindović

After a long night and a short sleep, we opened our eyes to the first day of competition. The same morning ritual and the journey to the Peace and Friendship Stadium were followed by the promised session of repairs to the robot, or more precisely to the ball-catching mechanism. After a short test session we changed our approach, opting for one similar to the solution of our colleagues from Norway. We built the ‘cage’ from aluminium profiles and shafts fitted with collars, which reduced friction and so let the ball glide along, turning freely on the shafts.
